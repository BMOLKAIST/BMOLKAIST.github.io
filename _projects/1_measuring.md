We develop **quantitative phase imaging (QPI)** and **holotomography (HT)** techniques — both the optical instrumentation and the reconstruction algorithms — to measure the amplitude and phase of light fields and to recover the three-dimensional refractive-index distribution of biological samples in a label-free, quantitative manner.

Recent directions include holotomography with visible LED illumination, dielectric-tensor tomography, and diffraction-tomography reconstruction algorithms.
