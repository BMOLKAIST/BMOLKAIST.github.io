We apply **machine learning** to optical information — for reconstruction, virtual staining, subcellular segmentation, and the discovery of imaging biomarkers from label-free refractive-index data. These approaches connect the quantitative signals of QPI and holotomography to biologically and clinically meaningful readouts.
