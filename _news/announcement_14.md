**SPIE News features Jimin Cho’s study on lipid storage in liver organoids**

SPIE News featured a _Biophotonics Discovery_ study by BMOL graduate student Jimin Cho and co-authors. The team used label-free holotomography to image the same living mouse liver organoids in 3D every six hours. The images revealed two distinct patterns of lipid accumulation: oleic acid primarily enlarged lipid droplets, while linoleic acid primarily increased their number. Congratulations to Jimin and all co-authors!
