Our paper, **"Design Principles for Light-Scattering Reservoir Computing at the Edge of Chaos,"** by Geon Kim and YongKeun Park, is now published open access in **Advanced Computing**, 1(1), e70012. The study maps three physical design axes of a reconfigurable light-scattering reservoir and identifies a memory-capacity peak at the edge of chaos. Congratulations, Geon!
